Understanding the Limitations of Midjourney V7

One of the biggest challenges I faced was understanding the limitations of Midjourney V7. I quickly realized that the model has a tendency to get stuck in certain patterns or styles, and it can be difficult to break free from those constraints. The Power of Specificity

I've found that specificity is key when it comes to crafting effective Midjourney V7 prompts. The more detailed and descriptive I am, the better the results tend to be. I've also discovered that using reference points can be incredibly helpful. This gives the model a clear understanding of the aesthetic and tone I'm aiming for, and the results are often remarkably accurate.
