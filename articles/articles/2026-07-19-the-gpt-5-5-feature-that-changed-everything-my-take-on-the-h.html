The Power of Fine-Tuning

One of the most significant advantages of GPT-5.5 is its ability to fine-tune its performance on specific tasks and datasets. But then I decided to try fine-tuning the model on a custom dataset of product descriptions that I'd curated myself. The difference was night and day - the output was suddenly more engaging, more descriptive, and more tailored to the specific products I was working with.
