My AI Art Journey: Harnessing the Power of Midjourney V7

My journey with Midjourney V7 began with a series of simple experiments, using the default prompts and settings to get a feel for the tool. One of my earliest successes was a piece called "Neon Noir", which I created using a combination of keywords and phrases that I'd gleaned from my research on film noir and synthwave music.

The Importance of Human Judgment

As I delved deeper into the world of AI art, I began to realize the importance of human judgment and curation. While Midjourney V7 is an incredibly powerful tool, it's not a replacement for human creativity and intuition - at least, not yet. I've lost count of the number of times I've generated a piece that looks amazing at first glance, only to realize that it's missing a crucial element or feels unbalanced in some way.
