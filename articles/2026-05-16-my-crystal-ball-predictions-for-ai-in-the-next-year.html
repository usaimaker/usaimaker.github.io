My Crystal Ball: Predictions for AI in the Next Year

I'm still reeling from the last project I worked on, where I used AI to generate ideas for a client's marketing campaign - it was a mixed bag, to say the least. The AI tool spat out some genuinely innovative concepts, but it also produced a ton of rubbish that I had to sift through, and that's when it hit me: AI is only as good as the person using it. My experience has taught me that human creativity and judgment are essential in getting the most out of these tools.
