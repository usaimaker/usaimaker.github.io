My Hands-On Comparison: DALL-E vs Midjourney V7

I had to experiment with different prompts to get the desired outcome, which was time-consuming but ultimately worth it.

My experience with Midjourney V7 was different - it seemed to be more forgiving when it came to input prompts, and the results were often more consistent. I asked it to generate an image of a "mountain landscape at sunset", and the result was breathtaking - the colors, the lighting, and the overall mood were all spot on. I was impressed by the level of detail and realism that Midjourney V7 was able to achieve, and I found myself using it more and more as the days went by.
