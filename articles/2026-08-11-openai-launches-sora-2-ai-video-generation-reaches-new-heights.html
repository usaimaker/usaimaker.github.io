OpenAI Launches Sora 2: AI Video Generation Reaches New Heights

OpenAI has launched Sora 2, the next generation of its AI video generation model, and the results are nothing short of breathtaking.

What's New in Sora 2

Sora 2 can generate up to 60 seconds of 4K video from a single text prompt. The quality is so high that in blind tests, 68% of viewers couldn't distinguish Sora-generated videos from real footage.

The model handles complex physics simulations, consistent character motion, and even lip-syncs dialogue with impressive accuracy. I generated a video of a chef cooking a meal, and the knife movements, ingredient transformations, and steam patterns were all physically accurate.

Pricing and Access

Sora 2 is available through OpenAI's API at $0.10 per second of generated video. ChatGPT Pro subscribers get 100 free seconds per month. There's also a waitlist for a consumer-facing app.

Creative Applications

Filmmakers are already using Sora 2 for storyboarding and concept visualization. Marketing agencies are creating ad campaigns at a fraction of traditional costs. And educators are generating custom video content for any topic imaginable.

The Competition

Google's Veo 3 and Runway's Gen-4 are the main competitors. While both are impressive, Sora 2 leads in consistency, motion quality, and overall realism. The AI video market is expected to reach $10 billion by 2027.
